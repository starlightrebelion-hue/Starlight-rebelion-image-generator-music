## Historia (metodo Doble Fondo)

**Tesis:** La despedida no es un adiós, sino el acto de dejar atrás lo que ya no puede salvarse, para seguir buscando lo que no se puede encontrar.

- **Plano A (superficie):** a drowned old neighbourhood at low tide, street lamps standing in black water, the top floors of old apartment blocks rising from the bay; luz Lateral de pantalla; objeto a brass diving lamp. the last light of the surface world, the illusion of control, the ritual of return
- **Plano B (lo que se esconde):** a cracked satellite dish in the sky, its signal still active; luz magenta pulse from the dish’s core, visible only in the rain; objeto a magenta pulse in the dish’s core, visible only in the rain. the signal of a memory not sent, the ghost of a message that never reached home
- **Tension:** Romance peligroso bajo neón
- **Motivos:** a brass diving lamp, a magenta pulse in the dish’s core, a cracked satellite dish in the sky
- **Ancla literal:** a brass diving lamp
- **Se descubre:** The magenta pulse in the dish’s core is not a malfunction—it’s the same color as the light that once shone from the family photo in her pocket, now hidden beneath her raincoat.
- **Traducciones:** Lunar Departure -> the final ascent from the drowned city; luna -> the ghostly glow of the megatowers on the horizon; salida -> surfacing from the black water
- **Prohibido:** a lunar surface; a spaceship launching from the moon; a moon in the sky; a character looking at the moon; a traditional departure scene
